What is boba

Boba, or bubble tea, is a popular Taiwanese drink invented in the 1980s, consisting of tea mixed with milk or fruit, often featuring chewy tapioca pearls made from cassava root. Served hot or cold, it is customizable with toppings like brown sugar syrup, grass jelly, or popping boba.
